# OMVCS Decision Register

This is a project-management index of unresolved normative decisions expressly listed in the Specs and additional questions recorded as Design Gaps. `DEC-*` entries preserve the listed specification questions; `DG-*` entries index discovered gaps. Entries do not propose answers.

`Blocks` identifies the earliest implementation milestone in [docs/milestones.md](milestones.md) affected by the decision. `LATER` means the first affected work is beyond the currently defined M0–M8 milestones; `NONE` means the listed decision does not block any currently defined milestone. Where a decision also affects later milestones, the earliest one is listed and downstream implications are noted briefly.

`DEC-*` entries remain **OPEN** unless resolved through an approved decision workflow. A `DG-*` entry may be **RESOLVED** when its approved decision is recorded in an ADR and the affected Specs are updated. Do not resolve an entry by editing this table alone.
